01 / About

A developer shaped
by curiosity and practice.

I enjoy turning an idea into a working product, learning what I need along the way and improving the result through real use.

I'm an emerging full-stack developer interested in building useful web and mobile applications. My journey into software development began in 2023, when my team entered the Ghana Science and Tech Explorer Prize Challenge (GSTEP) with an idea called Healthy Habits — a prenatal wellness concept for women in Ghana.

Our team had the idea, but we needed programming support. Through our coach, I was introduced to Techten Planets. I took responsibility for the programming side while my teammates focused on research and design. We developed the project, reached the GSTEP 2023 finals and demonstrated it to visitors using Expo Go on mobile and Expo on the web.

That experience started a longer learning journey. I continued developing my skills, moved from student trainee to Software Development Intern at Techten Planets in 2026, and began a BSc in Web Application Development at GCTU.

Build with purpose Start with the user problem and make the solution clear, useful and accessible.
