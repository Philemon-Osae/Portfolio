ClassHub 🎓

ClassHub: Secure Class Portal

Live · GCTU BWAD L100
HTMLCSSVanilla JSSupabasePostgresRow Level SecurityAuthStoragewa.me APIVercel
Tap for details

A class website I built for my own class, so WhatsApp stays for chat and ClassHub is the searchable archive. Students register once, then browse materials by course and week, past questions, announcements, the real teaching timetable with a live week banner, deadline countdowns, and send requests. Course reps get an upload center (files or Drive links), pinned announcements, deletion, “seen by” counts and one-tap Share to WhatsApp.

Secure by design: rep access is enforced on the server. A Postgres trigger checks each rep’s index and secret code, and row level security blocks everyone else, so it can’t be bypassed from the browser.

Healthy Habits 🤰

Healthy Habits: Prenatal Wellness for Ghana

Coming soon · Real impact
React NativeExpo SDK 52+expo-video v2React NavigationEAS Build APKOffline-first
Tap for details

A mobile app with safe, simple prenatal exercises for pregnant women in Ghana. It works 100% offline after install, so it reaches mothers from Accra to villages with limited access to physiotherapy, and can be shared by Bluetooth or ShareIt in clinics and CHPS compounds.

4 guided routines, 2 minutes each, safe in all trimesters: Stand & Stretch for lower back pain, Bend Backwards for posture and breathing, Knee Movements to reduce swelling, Leg Stretching for circulation. No login and no data collection.
